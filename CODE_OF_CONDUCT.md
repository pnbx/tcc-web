# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a volunteer project serving a community of players, many of
them teenagers. Disagreement is fine; contempt is not.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming someone's intent.
- Keep criticism about the code, the design or the decision — not the person.
- Respect that maintainers work on this in their own time.
- Do not post other people's private information, including player data from
  the live server.
- Keep discussion in English or Persian, whichever you write best.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised content or attention, doxxing,
threats, and publishing exploit details for the live server.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that break
these rules, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations. Report
problems privately to the maintainer (Discord / Telegram, or the contact on the
repository profile). Reports are handled discreetly.

This is a small project, so the policy is short: if a maintainer asks you to
stop, stop.
